# Gargi PaperKraft: B2B Export Lead Generation & Closing Playbook

**Objective:** Systematically identify, contact, and close 50–100+ qualified international paper bag buyers (supermarket chains, packaging wholesalers, food delivery brands) for container-load repeat orders (20ft / 40ft FCL).

---

## Strategy 1: The Customs Bill of Lading (B/L) Mining Engine

In global trade, every ocean container shipment requires a **Bill of Lading (B/L)** filed with customs authorities. This public data reveals **the exact corporate names of companies actively importing paper bags, their shipment frequency, container counts, and destination ports**.

### 1. The Platforms to Use
* **ImportYeti (Free / Freemium):** Excellent for US and international corporate lookups ([importyeti.com](https://www.importyeti.com)).
* **Volza (Paid / Free Trial):** Over 80+ countries' customs data; deep search by HS code and destination port ([volza.com](https://www.volza.com)).
* **Zauba / Eximpedia / Trademo:** Real-time Indian export bills of entry from Mundra, Nhava Sheva (JNPT), and ICD Kanpur.

### 2. Search Parameters
* **Primary HS Codes:**
  * **`4819.30.00`** – Sacks and bags having a base width >= 40 cm (Heavy grocery, industrial shopping, large delivery bags).
  * **`4819.40.00`** – Other sacks and bags, including paper carry bags, SOS takeaway bags, flat bottom shopping bags with handles.
  * **`4819.20.00`** – Folding cartons, boxes, and food containers of paper.
* **Target Destination Countries:**
  1. **United Arab Emirates (UAE):** Port of Jebel Ali (Dubai), Port of Khalifa (Abu Dhabi).
  2. **United Kingdom (UK):** Port of Felixstowe, London Gateway, Southampton.
  3. **Saudi Arabia (KSA):** King Abdulaziz Port (Dammam), Jeddah Islamic Port.
  4. **Spain:** Port of Barcelona, Port of Valencia.
  5. **Italy:** Port of Genoa, Port of La Spezia, Gioia Tauro.
  6. **Germany:** Port of Hamburg, Bremerhaven.

### 3. What Information to Extract
For every shipment found, record in a Google Sheet:
1. **Importer Name** (e.g., "XYZ Packaging Supplies Ltd", "Gulf Food Logistics LLC").
2. **Country & Port of Discharge** (e.g., Jebel Ali, Dubai).
3. **Annual Volume** (e.g., 6 x 40ft containers per year).
4. **Current Supplier Country** (e.g., China, Vietnam, Turkey).
5. **Product Description** (e.g., "Brown kraft SOS grocery bag 80 GSM").

## Strategy 4: The "Sample Box" Closing Engine

Never try to close a $40,000 container order on the first call. **Close the Sample Box first.**

### Why the Sample Box Works:
1. **Low Friction:** Asking a buyer for a shipping address is a micro-commitment with zero financial risk for them.
2. **Tactile Trust:** When the buyer holds your heavy-gauge kraft paper, tugs on the twisted rope handle, and sees crisp flexo ink, 80% of their doubts evaporate.
3. **Packaging the Box Professionally:**
   * Include 1 x SOS Small, 1 x SOS Medium, 1 x SOS Large, 1 x Flat Bottom Shopping Bag, 1 x Printed sample.
   * Include a printed **Product Specification Sheet** showing dimensions, GSM options, BF ratings, and container load counts (20ft FCL vs 40ft HC).
   * Include a cover letter addressed to the buyer.

## Strategy 5: Overcoming Buyer Objections & Scammer Fears

Foreign buyers are hesitant to send advance wire transfers to unknown suppliers in India. Here is how to eliminate their fear:

| Buyer Fear / Objection | How Gargi PaperKraft Solves It |
|---|---|
| *"Are you a real manufacturer or just an agent/trader?"* | **Offer an instant Live Video Tour:** "Let’s hop on a 5-minute WhatsApp video call right now; I will show you our paper reels, active machinery, and packing line in real time." |
| *"Will the bags tear during transit or heavy load?"* | "Our paper is sourced from FSC-certified mills with certified Burst Factor (BF 20 to 35+). We will include burst test reports with your dispatch." |
| *"What if quality changes between sample and container?"* | "We provide pre-shipment inspection photos, weight tally sheets, and third-party inspection (e.g. SGS / buyer's inspection agency) prior to stuffing." |
| *"How do payment terms work?"* | Offer standard export terms: **30% advance on order confirmation + 70% against copy of Bill of Lading (B/L) after container stuffing**, or Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) for multi-container contracts. |
